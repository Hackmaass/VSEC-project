💨 Atmospheric Wind Ventilation
r = -0.313
Statistically Significant (p < 0.001)

Higher horizontal wind velocities promote boundary-layer turbulence and mechanical advection, physically diluting ground-level particulate plumes away from human breathing zones.

🌧️ Monsoonal Wet Scavenging
r = -0.224
Hydrological Precipitation Washout

Falling raindrops capture airborne aerosols through inertial impaction and diffusion (wet scavenging), cleansing the lower troposphere and driving national PM2.5 to annual lows in July–August.

🌡️ Winter Thermal Inversion
r = -0.171
Boundary Layer Trapping Lid

Cold winter temperatures cause intense nocturnal radiative cooling of the ground. The resulting shallow temperature inversion lid caps the boundary layer, trapping emissions near the ground.
